Craft carried on the loom, not in a factory

Maniabandha is an ancestral, handwoven ikat tradition of Odisha. Every thread is tie-dyed and set on the handloom by hand, long before it becomes a saree — the same way it has been done in this family for generations.

The motifs draw from Jagannath temple iconography and Buddhist craft traditions native to the region — elephants, conch, lotus and temple forms bound into the weave itself, not printed on top of it.
